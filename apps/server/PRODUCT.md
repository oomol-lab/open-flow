# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and deployment operators who build, run, publish, and maintain typed workflows on infrastructure they control.

## Product Purpose

Open Flow Server is the self-hosted deployment and operator surface for Open Flow. It keeps workflow authoring, execution, operational state, and deployment-owned configuration in one understandable system.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, clear, and reliable. The interface should feel like a focused professional tool that makes operational state and available actions obvious.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style decoration, ornamental motion, unfamiliar controls, and important operator actions hidden behind unlabeled overflow menus. Do not imply that deployment Variables are an encrypted Secret Manager.

## Design Principles

- Keep the deployment boundary explicit.
- Make primary operator destinations discoverable.
- Prefer familiar controls and direct language.
- Keep visual and coded workflow behavior consistent.
- Show operational state without decorative noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the existing component conventions for keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, theme contrast, localization, and reduced-motion compatibility.
